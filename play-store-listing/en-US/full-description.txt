Design and build brick-built models in a touch-first 3D workspace on your Android phone.

BUILD IN 3D
• Place, move, rotate, tilt, recolor and delete individual parts.
• Snap standard bricks and plates to a stud grid.
• Select and edit parts in imported models.
• Choose from the LDraw parts and colors available on your device.

IMPORT AND EXPORT
• Import LDraw LDR and MPD files, BrickLink Studio IO models, and BrickBuilder project backups.
• IO compatibility varies by file; some metadata may remain uneditable.
• Import a BrickLink XML parts list and build using its listed quantities.
• Review and edit imported building steps when supported by the source file.
• Export LDraw files, BrickLink parts lists, and project backups.
• Create PDF building instructions.
• Render the current view as a transparent 4K PNG or vector SVG. Very complex SVG exports may be limited by device resources.

WORK OFFLINE
• Projects and autosaves are stored locally on your device.
• Installed part geometry remains available offline.
• Optional online downloads can retrieve missing LDraw geometry and are disabled by default. An imported model can be incomplete if its required geometry is unavailable.
