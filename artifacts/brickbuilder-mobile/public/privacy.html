2. Core principle: local processing

BrickBuilder is a 3D building application for brick models. No user account is required. The Android app processes projects and imported files on your device. The app does not automatically send your models or parts lists to me and does not automatically synchronize them to the cloud.

This Android version does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs and does not use its own service to automatically submit crash reports. It does not access contacts, location, camera, or microphone, and it does not read an advertising ID.

3. Data processed on your device

  • Project data: project names, local project IDs, save times, parts, positions, orientations, colors, and other model information.
  • Imported files: IO, LDR, MPD, JSON, XML, or CSV files that you select and their parsed contents. These may include original archives, file names, parts lists, quantities, and embedded metadata, such as author information.
  • Local work and library data: autosaves, required part geometry, installed part packages, catalog and color mappings, local settings, and technical cache data.
  • Export files: files created when saving or sharing, in the local app cache or at a location you select.

Whether this information contains personal data also depends on what you enter in project names or imported files. Avoid including personal information that is not needed for building.

Data is stored, among other places, in the Android WebView's local database (IndexedDB) and app-specific file storage. I cannot access this local content remotely.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Local data is used to display and edit models, import and export files, manage parts lists, reopen projects, and save building progress automatically.

Where personal data is processed to provide app functions you request, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Where applicable, technically necessary storage and access on your device is based on section 25(2)(2) TDDDG. No tracking cookies are used.

If you contact me, I process the information you provide to handle your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for service-related requests or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise, based on my legitimate interest in responding. Statutory retention duties may require processing under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

5. Internet connections and external services

The Android version loads bundled app, catalog, and geometry data from the app package or local storage. A local WebView address is not a publicly accessible server. Unless online loading is enabled, the geometry feature does not connect to my server.

Optional geometry download: In “Privacy (Android)” you can enable “Load missing geometry online.” Only then, when importing or building, the app requests LDraw files that are not available locally, required subfiles, and license texts over HTTPS from https://brickbuilder.blackfishbox.de/api. Requests are sent to me as the service operator and to the technical hosting provider Ultahost. The server is located in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Requests contain the required LDraw file paths, not your project files, local file names, project names, colors, quantities, or complete parts lists. The sequence of requested parts may still reveal information about a model.

These connections technically process your public IP address, request time, requested path including the part identifier, and any technical information supplied by the network stack. The server may log requests and errors, such as response status and transferred data volume. The download feature does not add analytics IDs or advertising IDs. Mobile data charges may apply.

Enabling this optional feature is your consent to the download and associated data transfer (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can disable it in the same dialog at any time for future requests. Requests already sent may still reach the server. Downloaded and verified geometry remains stored locally for offline use. Local features remain available without consent, but geometry that is not installed locally cannot be displayed.

Where technical server logs are needed for security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention, they are processed based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is secure and reliable operation of the download service. Log data is not used for advertising or user profiling. The server's access and error logs are retained for 30 days and then deleted. Withdrawal stops future downloads but does not automatically remove server logs that were lawfully collected before the end of this period.

If you open an external link, for example to a source or license, your browser may connect to that provider. The provider usually receives technical connection data such as your IP address and applies its own privacy policy. Opening a link does not automatically send your models.

6. Import, export, and sharing

Consent to optional online loading also covers missing geometry for visible preview images in the full-catalog search. These files are loaded one at a time from the server described in section 5 and stored locally. The selected color is rendered on your device and is not transmitted. Without consent, Android previews use only locally available geometry.

The app processes files that you select through the file picker. You decide which files to provide. A document provider you choose may be a cloud storage service; its own privacy terms apply.

An export is first created in the local app cache. Android then opens its save or share dialog. Another app or service receives access to the export only if you choose it. Depending on the format, an export may contain the model, metadata, and retained original content. Review it before sharing.

BrickBuilder does not itself transfer your projects to a cloud service operated by me. Transfers through an email, messenger, or cloud app you choose are handled by that provider.

7. Google Play and Android

If you install or update the app through Google Play, Google processes data as part of its own services. Android may also process system diagnostics or other platform data according to your device settings. This is separate from BrickBuilder's local model processing.

The app does not require an account with its own service and does not integrate its own Google Analytics or advertising measurement. More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Retention, deletion, and backups

Local projects and library data generally remain until you delete them, reset the app data in Android, or uninstall the app. The operating system may clear cache data or storage earlier in some circumstances. The app does not guarantee a retention period and I do not manage a recovery copy.

Manage saved projects and installed packages through “Manage offline storage.” To fully delete app data, use the Android settings to clear the app's storage or data. The exact wording depends on your device. This removes local projects and autosaves.

Export files in the app cache are not automatically deleted immediately after each share. They may be removed by clearing the app cache, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. You must delete separately saved exports, sent files, and copies in other apps or services yourself. Back up important projects before resetting app data.

If you email me, I retain the message for as long as needed to handle the request and, where relevant, protect my legitimate interests. It is then deleted unless statutory retention duties or unresolved claims require otherwise.

9. Security and recipients

The app uses Android's protections for app-specific storage. No additional custom encryption of project data or export files is promised. Protect your device with a screen lock and remember that unlocked devices, exports you share, and other apps may create their own risks.

Because projects are processed locally, they are not automatically sent to a hosting, advertising, or analytics service operated by me. When geometry download is enabled, I receive the request data described in section 5; my hosting provider receives connection data needed for technical operation. Other recipients may include document or parts services you explicitly choose. If you contact me, your message is sent to me and to the email services used to handle it. Technical access by those services cannot generally be ruled out.

Local model processing does not transfer data to me in a country outside the European Economic Area. For external services you choose, refer to their information about recipients, storage locations, and international transfers.

2. Grundprinzip: lokale Verarbeitung

BrickBuilder ist eine 3D-Konstruktionsanwendung für Klemmbausteinmodelle. Für ihre Nutzung ist kein Benutzerkonto erforderlich. Die Android-App verarbeitet Projekte und importierte Dateien auf deinem Gerät. Es gibt keine automatische Übertragung deiner Modelle oder Teilelisten an mich und keine automatische Cloud-Synchronisierung durch die App.

Die hier beschriebene Android-Fassung enthält keine eingebundenen Werbe-, Tracking- oder Analyse-SDKs und keinen eigenen Dienst zur automatischen Übermittlung von Absturzberichten. Sie greift nicht auf Kontakte, Standort, Kamera oder Mikrofon zu und liest keine Werbe-ID aus.

3. Welche Daten auf deinem Gerät verarbeitet werden

  • Projektdaten: Projektname, lokale Projektkennungen, Speicherzeitpunkte, Bauteile, Positionen, Ausrichtungen, Farben und sonstige Modellinformationen.
  • Importierte Dateien: von dir ausgewählte IO-, LDR-, MPD-, JSON-, XML- oder CSV-Dateien und ihre ausgelesenen Inhalte. Dazu können Originalarchive, Dateinamen, Teilelisten, Mengen und enthaltene Metadaten gehören. Metadaten können beispielsweise Autorenangaben enthalten.
  • Lokale Arbeits- und Bibliotheksdaten: automatische Speicherstände, benötigte Teile-Geometrien, installierte Teilepakete, Katalog- und Farbzuordnungen sowie lokale Einstellungen und technische Cache-Daten.
  • Exportdateien: beim Speichern oder Teilen erzeugte Dateien im lokalen App-Cache beziehungsweise an dem von dir gewählten Speicherort.

Ob diese Inhalte personenbezogene Daten enthalten, hängt auch davon ab, welche Angaben du in Projektnamen oder importierten Dateien verwendest. Vermeide darin personenbezogene Informationen, die für das Bauen nicht erforderlich sind.

Die Speicherung erfolgt unter anderem in der lokalen Datenbank der Android-WebView (IndexedDB) und im appbezogenen Dateispeicher. Ich kann diese lokalen Inhalte nicht aus der Ferne einsehen.

4. Zwecke und Rechtsgrundlagen

Die lokalen Daten werden verwendet, um Modelle darzustellen und zu bearbeiten, Dateien zu importieren und zu exportieren, Teilelisten zu verwalten, Projekte wieder zu öffnen und den Baufortschritt automatisch zu sichern.

Technisch notwendiges Speichern und Auslesen auf deinem Gerät erfolgt, soweit § 25 TDDDG anwendbar ist, auf Grundlage von § 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG. Es werden keine Tracking-Cookies eingesetzt.

Wenn du mich kontaktierst, verarbeite ich deine Angaben zur Bearbeitung der Anfrage.
